## Resolved decisions (2026-09-28, Sivert's master execution instruction)

- **D-1 Platform:** evaluate on actual requirements, not old-site compatibility. Result: standalone TypeScript application (Astro + Preact + framework-free core + Three.js), commerce behind a provider boundary. Full comparison in [ADR-001](adr-001-platform.md).
- **D-2 Launch:** the website does not need to wait for every advanced Signgen feature; site and Skaperverksted are developed in parallel; nothing that does not exist is faked.
- **D-3 Vector rule:** functional programmatic geometry is allowed (grids, rulers, measurement marks, construction lines, Bézier curves, geometry paths, editor overlays, snapping guides, technical visualisation). The restriction covers arbitrary decorative vector artwork and invented decorative SVG iconography.
- **D-4 Export:** Signgen supports real STL and 3MF export; it is not order-only. Commercial-use restrictions, pricing, subscription tiers and free/paid limits are not locked and must not be invented.
- **Assets:** missing logo, mascot and photography must not stop work; use clearly identified temporary placeholders that can be replaced cleanly; prefer existing project assets.

## Facts that need confirmation before they are published

Not blocking implementation; content stays out until confirmed.

- Capabilities: UV printing (asked in August, never answered), 3D scanning and reproduction, resin printing, jewellery.
- Materials and colours actually stocked; printers and multi-colour capacity (the brief mentions about four colours).
- Prices, delivery times, response time for enquiries, what an enquiry costs, whether a reprint round is included, whether customers keep their files.
- Real contact details, opening hours, organisation details.
- Real customer reviews and references.
- Lamp fittings (for example E27) and electrical safety wording. E27 42 mm and E14 29 mm are common values, marked `vanlig-mal` and adjustable per design.
- Bambu Lab LED Lamp Kit 001: module height (18 mm in the product data we could read; Bambu Lab sources disagree), cable exit, cable diameter, plug size, screw positions. The official store page and Bambu Lab's design guide PDF could not be read from the build environment on 2026-09-29; values are taken from Bambu Lab's product data as quoted by retailers (sources in `generatorer/lampe/maskinvare.ts`).
- Lamp fit tolerances (0.3 mm recess clearance, 0.5 mm lip, 20 mm cable hole, 7 × 5 mm channel): Designporten design defaults until a test print of the fit-test piece has been made.
- The dragon generator part library: who models the parts and in which tool.
- BuildForge mechanics (handoff §17): which switch model, its measured housing, stem and travel, the printer, material and orientation for the first test, clearances from a calibration print, the top's mass limit from a return test, the intended age and use, and who approves physical tests. Until then the only profile is `mx-lignende-prove` 0.1.0, a trial profile: 14 mm cutout, 1.5 mm plate and 4 mm travel are common published MX values (`vanlig-mal`); housing size, stem height, cross size, socket depth, clearance 0.4 mm and the top limits are trial values (`proveverdi`). All are shown with their certainty in the editor and the report.
- BuildForge joints (also for modular models): the peg fit (`figurledd-prove` 0.1.0: 5 mm peg, 0.2 mm radial clearance, 6 mm hole, 0.6 mm gap, 0.5 mm end clearance — trial values and design choices), whether friction holds the parts, and the print orientation of each part. Needs a test print of one joint pair before anything is called working.
- BuildForge ball joints (`kuleledd-prove` 0.1.0: 8 mm ball, 3.6 mm neck, 0.2 mm radial clearance, 7 mm socket mouth, 1.6 mm wall, 0.6 mm gap, 25° maximum bend — trial values and design choices): whether the socket snaps over the ball, holds a pose by friction, prints without supports, and survives repeated bending. Needs a test print before any value is presented as working.
