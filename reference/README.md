# Reference material

Inspiration and preserved prototypes. Nothing here is shipped, and nothing here overrides the [master specification](../DESIGNPORTEN_MASTER.md). When a reference and the spec disagree, the spec wins; conflicts already resolved are listed in the [decision log](../docs/decisions/decision-log.md#conflicts-resolved).

All concept images are AI-generated mock-ups posted by Sivert on 2026-09-28 (converted from PNG to WebP). They show no real products, customers or staff, and their prices, ratings, contact details and quotes are invented.
